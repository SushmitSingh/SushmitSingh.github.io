1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Welcome to Alarm Jail ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by ToonCoder. Alarm Jail is an anti-oversleep utility alarm clock engineered specifically for heavy sleepers, featuring wake-up puzzle missions, volume lock protocols, and a multi-layer fail-safe architecture.

We take user privacy exceptionally seriously. Alarm Jail does not collect, sell, or rent your personal information. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or transmit private details such as your name, email address, contacts, or calendar schedules to any cloud server. Your alarm configurations, custom schedules, habit streaks, and mission progress stay 100% locally on your physical device.

2. Information Collection & Usage

2.1 Data Handled Exclusively On-Device (Zero Remote Uploads)

The following data is created during your use of Alarm Jail and is stored strictly within your device's private, encrypted internal storage (SQLite / Jetpack Room / EncryptedSharedPreferences):

  • Alarm Schedules & Settings: Alarm trigger times, repeat days, custom labels, and vibration patterns.
  • Disarm Mission Preferences: Selected challenges (Math equations, Typing tests, Timed Hard mode, Missing letter, Memory match, etc.).
  • Wake Analytics & Streaks: Weekly/monthly wake timestamps, disarm response speeds, and consecutive consistency streaks displayed in your Briefing screen.

This data is never sent to any remote servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

2.2 Automatically Collected Diagnostic & Advertising Data

To provide the App for free and diagnose stability issues, Alarm Jail incorporates industry-standard Google Play Services and Google AdMob SDK. These SDKs may collect pseudonymous and non-personal diagnostic metrics, such as:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version (e.g. Android 14/15/16).
  • General, non-precise location inferred from IP address (country or city level, for ad localization and language rendering).
  • Google Advertising Identifier (GAID), which can be reset or opted-out by you at any time in Android system settings.
  • App performance logs, crash stack traces, and ad interaction metrics (e.g., ad impressions and clicks).

3. Android Permissions & Technical Rationale

To guarantee that alarms trigger reliably without failure under Android Doze mode, aggressive OEM battery management, and screen-lock states, Alarm Jail requests the following specific permissions:

SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM Core Utility

Mandatory for Android 12+ (API 31+) to guarantee your alarms fire at the exact second scheduled, bypassing Doze mode batching and power-saving delays.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Core Utility

Allows the alarm siren audio to continue playing continuously and reliably while presenting full-screen interactive disarm missions without being killed by the Android OS.

USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT & SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Core Utility

Allows Alarm Jail to immediately display the emergency wake-up and challenge screen directly over your lock screen and on top of any active background apps when an alarm triggers.

REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Reliability Layer

Used to request an exemption from OEM battery killers (Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Huawei, etc.) so that background alarms are never silenced unexpectedly.

ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY Emergency Override

Allows emergency alarms to bypass Do Not Disturb (DND) modes when you explicitly enable the emergency volume lock feature.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & QUICKBOOT_POWERON Auto-Recovery

Ensures all scheduled upcoming alarms are automatically recalculated and re-registered with the Android AlarmManager immediately after your phone restarts.

CAMERA, SENSORS, & NFC (Optional Missions) Optional Disarm Missions

Only requested if you choose to activate specific physical disarm missions (such as Step Counter, Shake, QR Code, or NFC scan). Crucial note: Sensor data and camera frames are processed 100% locally on-device in real-time. No photos or sensor streams are ever recorded, saved to your gallery, or uploaded to any server.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not store your data on remote servers, you have complete control over your information at all times:

  • Delete within App: You can edit or delete individual alarms and reset streak statistics directly within the app interface.
  • Clear App Storage: You can permanently wipe all local database records by going to Android Settings → Apps → Alarm Jail → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Alarm Jail automatically purges all stored databases, configurations, and cached files from your device.
