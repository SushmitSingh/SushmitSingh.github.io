1. Introduction & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which ToonCoder ("we", "our", or "us") manages and protects the data handled by the NetTerm Android mobile application (Package ID: com.tooncoder.netterm).

NetTerm is an advanced, hardware-level network traffic inspector, real-time URL sniffer, and local DNS sinkhole firewall designed for developers, network engineers, system administrators, and security-conscious individuals.

Our foundational guarantee is straightforward: NetTerm operates strictly on-device. We do NOT operate remote proxy servers, remote VPN gateways, or cloud databases. We do NOT harvest, sell, analyze, or monetize your network traffic or personal information.

2. Android VpnService Disclosure & Architecture

Google Play VpnService Compliance Declaration

NetTerm utilizes Android's official android.net.VpnService API solely to establish an internal, loopback virtual network interface (tun0) on your local device. No remote VPN servers exist. Your internet traffic is NOT redirected to external servers.

Why NetTerm Uses VpnService:

  • Local Packet Header Inspection: To read local IP, TCP, UDP, HTTP, HTTPS (TLS Server Name Indication), and DNS packet headers directly on your device without requiring root access.
  • Hardware Wire-Speed Bypass: NetTerm implements allowBypass() so that non-DNS/non-intercepted payload data flows directly to your actual network gateway with 0% bottleneck and full wire speed.
  • Local DNS Sinkholing (Firewall): When you block data-hogging domains or advertising trackers, NetTerm's local DNS engine resolves them to 0.0.0.0 locally on your phone, blocking requests before cellular data is spent.

Zero Remote Tunneling: Unlike commercial VPN providers that route your traffic through remote overseas data centers, NetTerm acts as a local packet filter. Your packets travel directly from your phone to your destination network.

4. Local Storage & User Control

Any data generated by NetTerm—including session bandwidth logs, blocked URL lists, and onboarding acknowledgments—is stored in your device's sandboxed private storage directory:

  • Log Storage: Live URL inspection entries are retained in an in-memory cyclic buffer (last 500 entries) and discarded upon service stop.
  • Calendar Historical Aggregates: Daily data usage counters are stored locally in Android's encrypted app storage.
  • Immediate Deletion: You have full control to clear all session logs and statistics at any time via the ⚡ Hub -> Clear Logs button, or by clearing app data in Android Settings.
