2 Information We Collect & How It Is Collected

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. The App is designed so that you can study, solve mock tests, and practice offline without requiring sensitive personal identity submissions.

  • Local Device Storage Data (Non-Personal): The App uses an on-device local database (Room/SQLite) to store your study preferences (selected language Hindi/English), quiz history, mock test scores, question bookmarks, and chapter reading progress. This data remains on your physical smartphone and is not uploaded to our external servers.
  • Anonymous Diagnostic & Device Information: Automatically provided by your Android device when interacting with Google Play Services and AdMob, including device model, operating system version, network state, and pseudonymous advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID - GAID).

3 Information We DO NOT Collect

To provide a safe and private exam preparation experience, we explicitly DO NOT collect, store, sell, or solicit:

  • ❌ Your Government ID documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, UP Police Roll Numbers).
  • ❌ Precise Real-time GPS Location tracking.
  • ❌ Camera, Microphone, Audio recordings, or Photo Gallery files.
  • ❌ Phone Contacts, Call Logs, or SMS contents.
  • ❌ Financial, Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or Payment Information (The App is 100% Free).
  • ❌ Biometric identification data.

5 Android App Permissions Disclosures

The App requests only standard Android permissions required to load dynamic quizzes, exam updates, and ads:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to fetch updated question banks, news articles, and display AdMob ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify network connectivity and seamlessly enable Offline Mode when disconnected.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Optional permission to deliver daily mock test reminders, exam date alerts, and UP Police syllabus updates. You can disable notifications anytime in system settings.

6 Data Retention, User Control & Deletion

Because all test results, bookmark items, and quiz progress are stored locally on your device in private SQLite app sandboxes:

  • Clear All Data: You can delete all your stored scores and question bookmarks at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android: Settings > Apps > Police Prep > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling: Completely removes all locally retained app files from your smartphone immediately.
  • Account Deletion: The App does not require mandatory account registration to access study material. If you have registered a phone profile, you may request complete profile record removal by emailing us at policeprep@tooncoder.com.

7 Children's Privacy Protection

Our services are intended for general adult and young adult competitive exam candidates (typically aged 18 to 28 years preparing for police constable eligibility). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to purge such data.
